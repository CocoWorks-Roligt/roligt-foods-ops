# Cheat sheet — QC analyst

*Applies when the new look (Pipeline) is switched on. Your pages: Quality Control, Control
Samples, Test Parameters.*

## Open first
**Pipeline** → the QC tile counts records awaiting a verdict (oldest first); the Control
samples tile counts kept samples inside their 7-day window.

## Your loop
1. **Work the bench** — Quality → *Test entry*: pick or scan (batch or QC id) the oldest
   record; one card per required test; the **disposition is computed, never picked** — the
   preview shows the exact sentence the save will stamp. Save → the next-oldest opens.
2. **What a verdict does** — Released/Rejected moves stock in place; anything else sends it
   back to awaiting QC. A hold-area refusal is a toast from the save — the form stays open.
3. **Lab reports & evaluations** — stay on the register's *Review* (upload + generated
   PDFs attach there). Raising records for old batches is register-side too.
4. **Destroy samples on time** — Control Samples → *Destruction shelf*: nearest expiry
   first, past-expiry flagged; today's date pre-filled; collected-by stays on record.

## Words to know
- **Hold / Passed / Failed / Rework** — a held or unreleased lot cannot be blended, packed
  or dispatched; the app enforces it, you only decide.
- **Quarantine** = stock standing in a hold area awaiting your verdict or a write-off.
- Sample **retention** = 7 days from the day it was produced (configurable).

## Gotchas
- A batch with two QC records: finishing one says the sibling stays queued.
- You cannot move stock out of a hold area by verdict alone — Store does the move.
