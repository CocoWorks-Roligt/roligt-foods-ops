# Cheat sheet — Delivery agent

> **Not live yet.** The delivery app (a phone-first PWA) arrives with Shipment Tracking in
> the overhaul's Phase 4. This page says what it will ask of you, so the role is
> understood from day one.

## What you will see (when it ships)
- **Only your assigned shipments** — nothing else in the app is visible to you: no other
  customers, no stock, no costs.
- Each shipment: items and quantities, packaging (boxed/loose, carton count), customer
  address and contact, notes.

## What you will do
1. **Start delivery** — stamps *Out for Delivery* (time, optional location).
2. **Confirm per line** — full, partial, or rejected/damaged with a reason and optional
   photo. A rejection creates a Return record — QC decides what happens to the goods, not
   you.
3. **Take the signature** — customer signs on your phone screen and prints their name and
   phone number.
4. **The challan is generated for you** — a numbered delivery challan PDF with the
   signature embedded. It is immutable; a correction is a new linked version with a reason.

## Words to know
- **Challan** = the delivery note that travels with the goods.
- **Offline** — the app keeps your entries on the phone and syncs when the signal returns;
   the sync state is always shown. Nothing needs to be typed twice.

## Until then
Dispatch is closed out by Store/Dispatch; your role account will be created when the
shipment work goes live.
