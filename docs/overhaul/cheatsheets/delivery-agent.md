# Cheat sheet — Delivery agent

> **Live with Shipment Tracking (Phase 4).** Your app is *My Deliveries* on your phone —
> that is the entire app for this role. Install it from the browser menu (Share → Add to
> Home Screen on iPhone; the install prompt on Android).

## What you see
- **Only your assigned trips** — nothing else in the app is visible to you: no other
  customers, no stock, no costs, not even another agent's trip numbers.
- Each trip: the customer(s) and stop addresses, vehicle, the lines with their lots and
  quantities, and the packaging mode.
- Works with no signal: the day's list and everything you record stay on the phone and
  sync when the signal returns. The sync count is always shown at the top.

## What you do
1. **Start delivery** — stamps *Out for Delivery* with the time (location shared if the
   phone offers it). If the office forgot to mark the trip dispatched, this still gets
   you moving — you are never stranded.
2. **Confirm per line** — delivered and rejected/damaged counts. A rejection needs a
   reason and can take a photo. It becomes a Return record — QC decides what happens to
   the goods, not you.
3. **Take the signature** — the customer signs on your phone screen and prints their
   name and phone number. If the customer asks for OTP verification, unfold it and type
   the code.
4. **Submit** — one press records the delivery. The challan (the numbered delivery note
   with the signature in it) is generated for you; the office can print or share it. It
   never changes — a correction is a new version with a reason, and that is the office's
   job, not yours.

## Words to know
- **Challan** = the delivery note that travels with the goods.
- **Return** = what a rejected line becomes, so QC can put the stock back, hold it, or
  write it off.
- **Offline** — nothing needs to be typed twice. Entries wait on the phone ("2 deliveries
  wait to sync") and go out on their own.

## Rules of thumb
- Never guess a quantity: if delivered + rejected is more than what left the gate, the
  phone will refuse the line until it reconciles.
- Your account is an external one — the office creates it and assigns your trips
  (agent = your email).
